# Code of Conduct

This project follows the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**. The full text is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>, and it applies to every space of the
project: the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and any place where someone speaks
for it.

In short: be kind, assume good faith, keep to the technical question, and do not harass anyone. Insults,
personal attacks, discrimination, and publishing other people's private information are not welcome.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks the Code of Conduct, report it privately to the maintainer
at te.r@northeastern.edu, with `shale conduct` in the subject. All reports are read and handled
confidentially, and in good faith. The consequences are the ones the Covenant describes in its
enforcement guidelines, from a private warning to a permanent ban, in proportion to what happened.
